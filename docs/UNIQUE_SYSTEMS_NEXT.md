# Signature Systems — next build targets

## 1. Rumor Network
Information should travel through people, not omniscient UI. A server-side rumor object has source confidence, decay, districts, and eligible social routes. Players receive paraphrased rumors through contacts, bars, workplaces, informants, and community events.

## 2. Scar Tissue
Major events leave subtle physical/economic history: a robbed store repairs in stages, a burned property gets inspections, a neighborhood vigil can improve trust, repeated violence raises insurance and security costs.

## 3. Opportunity Director
Instead of fixed robbery cooldowns, a server director evaluates police coverage, district pressure, business supply, recent scene volume and character history to create **different opportunities for different people**. It never guarantees success or tells players exact hidden scores.

## 4. Witness Memory
NPC witness data can have confidence, viewing angle, lighting, distance, stress and later lineup reliability. No magical perfect descriptions.

## 5. Social Capital
Access comes from relationships and reliability. A person who burns every contact stops receiving premium opportunities even if they grinded money.

## 6. Business Supply Web
Restaurants need suppliers; garages need parts; clubs need beverage/entertainment contracts; hospitals need medical supply deliveries. Demand and failure ripple through the player economy.

## 7. Court Consequence Graph
A ruling can affect licenses, business permits, probation restrictions, sealed records, restitution, property claims, injunctions, and future case credibility.

## 8. Staff Story Console
Staff see unresolved scene threads and can seed believable follow-ups without knowing or forcing a desired ending.
